Privacy Policy

Last updated on November 12, 2026. This Privacy Policy details the types of information App Icon Studio ("we", "our", or "the Service", available at https://www.appicon.site) collects, how that information is used and protected, and your rights regarding your personal data and advertising preferences.

1. Client-Side Image Processing & Asset Privacy

Your graphic assets and uploaded images are processed locally within your browser using client-side JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API whenever possible.

  • Uploaded source files (PNG, JPG, SVG, WebP) are processed directly in your device's memory for icon generation, resizing, cropping, and compression.
  • We do not sell, license, harvest, or claim copyright over any images or logos you upload to the studio.
  • Temporary project records stored in local storage remain on your device and are never broadcasted to external marketing lists.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

We monetize our free services by displaying advertisements provided by Google AdSense and its certified advertising partners. In accordance with Google Publisher Policies, we disclose the following:

  • Third-Party Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to you based on your visits to App Icon Studio and other properties on the web.
  • Opting Out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or Your Online Choices.
  • Google Partner Data Policy: For detailed transparency on how Google collects and processes data when you use partner sites, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Cookie Usage & Consent Management (GDPR & UK)

Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help enhance your browsing experience, maintain session preferences, and deliver relevant ad experiences.

  • Essential / Technical Cookies: Necessary for core site features, layout preferences, and UI responsiveness.
  • Analytics Storage: Used to understand general traffic aggregates, browser versions, and device viewports without collecting sensitive personal details.
  • Advertising & Targeting Cookies: Placed by Google AdSense and authorized ad networks to evaluate ad performance and deliver relevant promotions.
  • Consent Management: For visitors located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we respect your consent choices via Google-certified Consent Management Platforms (CMP). You can review or modify your consent settings at any time through your browser's cookie settings.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to specific disclosures:

  • We do not sell personal information for monetary payment.
  • You have the right to request disclosure of what personal information is collected, used, or shared.
  • You have the right to request deletion of any personal information collected from you.
  • You have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your CCPA privacy rights.
  • To submit a verifiable consumer request, please contact us at contact@appicon.site.

Like most internet services, our hosting infrastructure (Vercel and edge content delivery networks) may automatically record standard technical log entries when you access the site:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address (anonymized in analytics).
  • Browser type, language, and operating system.

  • Date and time stamps for system monitoring and DDoS protection.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

App Icon Studio is a developer utility designed for software engineers, product designers, and general web creators. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to our service, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.
