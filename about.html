About App Icon Studio

Streamlining Mobile & Desktop App Icon Preparation

App Icon Studio is a specialized web-based developer tool engineered by Rosdeb Koch and published under Pilach. Built for indie developers, mobile engineers, UI/UX designers, and software agencies, it transforms a single high-resolution master asset into production-ready icon bundles for iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows.

What the Product Does

Upload a single 1024×1024 master image and immediately generate all platform-compliant asset sets: Apple AppIcon catalogs with Contents.json, Android adaptive icons with mipmap densities, Play Store 512px assets, macOS .icns sets, and Windows high-DPI .ico bundles.

Our Mission & Why It Exists

Manually resizing dozens of individual square files for Xcode asset catalogs and Android Studio resource directories is tedious, repetitive, and prone to padding or cropping errors. App Icon Studio was created to make icon generation instant, visual, and completely private directly in your browser.

Privacy First Architecture

Unlike legacy online converters that send your proprietary graphics to remote servers, App Icon Studio utilizes modern in-browser HTML5 Canvas APIs and Web Workers. Your images remain in your local browser memory and are never uploaded, logged, or sold.

Publisher & Editorial Standards

Pilach maintains App Icon Studio as an active, curated web resource. In addition to our generator and image manipulation utilities, we author comprehensive technical documentation, platform sizing specifications, and app store design guides to help builders succeed.

Contact & Creator Information

The platform is actively maintained by developer Rosdeb Koch. For suggestions, feature requests, or technical support, reach out directly through our Contact Page or email us at support@appicon.site.

Commitment to Quality

We continuously test our export packages against the latest Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Google Material Design specifications so your apps can pass App Store Review and Google Play Console verification without rejection.
