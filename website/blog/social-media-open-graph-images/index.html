Designing Social Share Cards: Open Graph & Twitter Card Specifications

Stop messy feed previews: master the 1200x630px standard, safe title zones, metadata tags, and cache debugging for Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, and Slack.

Published November 2026 • 6 min read • App Icon Studio Engineering Team

⚡ Key Takeaways

  • The universal standard for modern social share cards is 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio).
  • Keep critical typography, logos, and focal subjects within the central 1000x500px 'safe zone' to prevent platform clipping.
  • Serve WebP or high-quality JPEG under 300KB to ensure instant rendering across social app scrapers.
  • Always specify both twitter:card and og:image metadata tags in your HTML document head.

Why Open Graph Cards Control Your Viral Distribution

Whenever someone shares a link to your website, article, or SaaS tool on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Slack, iMessage, or WhatsApp, the platform's automated web crawler visits your URL, parses your metadata, and generates an interactive rich preview card. Studies show that links with rich image preview cards receive up to 300% more engagements and clicks compared to plain text links.

However, if your Open Graph image uses the wrong aspect ratio, file size, or dimensions, the platform may crop off your headline, distort your logo, or refuse to display the card entirely.

18

The Definitive 1200×630 Specification

Dimensions: 1200 pixels wide by 630 pixels tall.

Aspect Ratio: Exactly 1.905:1 (commonly referenced as 1.91:1).

File Size Target: Under 300 KB (WhatsApp and Twitter crawlers frequently discard images exceeding 1 MB).

The Safe Margin: Leave at least 60px of padding around all four edges. On mobile screens, social platforms often crop the outer perimeter or superimpose branding watermarks and domain pills in the lower corners.

Complete HTML Meta Tag Implementation

To ensure universal compatibility across all major platforms, include these standardized tags in your HTML <head>:

<meta property="og:type" content="article">
<meta property="og:title" content="Your Compelling Article Title">
<meta property="og:description" content="Engaging summary under 160 characters.">
<meta property="og:image" content="https://www.example.com/assets/og-card.jpg">
<meta property="og:image:width" content="1200">
<meta property="og:image:height" content="630">

<!-- Twitter / X Card Specification -->
<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image">
<meta name="twitter:title" content="Your Compelling Article Title">
<meta name="twitter:image" content="https://www.example.com/assets/og-card.jpg">
