Understanding Android Adaptive Icon Safe Zones

An adaptive icon layout has total dimensions of 108×108dp. However, device OEM launchers clip this canvas using various shapes. To ensure your artwork or logo never gets trimmed by a circular mask (like on Pixel phones) or a squircle mask (like on Samsung Galaxy phones), always keep your critical brand symbol centered within the inner 72×72dp safe zone. App Icon Studio's padding controls make this easy.

Step-by-Step Android Studio Integration Guide

1. Generate & Download ZIP

In the App Icon Studio editor, upload your logo, choose an appropriate background color and safe-zone padding, and select the Android platform before clicking "Generate Export Plan".

2. Copy to Resource Folder

Extract the ZIP archive. Inside the android/ directory, you will find the mipmap-* folders. Copy these directly into your project's app/src/main/res/ directory.

3. Verify AndroidManifest.xml

Ensure your AndroidManifest.xml points to the icon: android:icon="@mipmap/ic_launcher" and android:roundIcon="@mipmap/ic_launcher_round".

Android Icon FAQs

What are Android adaptive icons and why are they required?

Introduced in Android 8.0 (API level 26), adaptive icons allow OEM device launchers (like Samsung OneUI, Google Pixel Launcher, Xiaomi MIUI) to apply custom mask shapes—such as circles, rounded squares, squirrels, or teardrops—to launcher icons dynamically, accompanied by parallax and motion effects.

What is the key safe zone for Android adaptive icons?

An adaptive icon canvas is 108×108dp. However, the outer 18dp on each side is reserved for masking and parallax animations. The critical safe zone where your logo or visual mark must stay is the inner 66dp or 72dp diameter circle.

What are the exact Android mipmap density resolutions?

Android defines standard launcher resolutions across density buckets: mdpi (48×48px), hdpi (72×72px), xhdpi (96×96px), xxhdpi (144×144px), and xxxhdpi (192×192px). Additionally, Google Play Console requires a 512×512px 32-bit PNG icon.

Can I use transparent PNG files for Google Play icons?

Yes, Google Play 512×512px icons can support transparency, but Google automatically applies a subtle drop shadow and rounded square corner mask in the store. Using a full solid background often provides more consistent brand presentation.
