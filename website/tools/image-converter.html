Private In-Browser Image Format Converter

Different digital platforms and web frameworks require specific image formats for optimal performance, quality, and transparency. Converting graphics with our client-side utility gives you granular control over container formats, quality tiers, and background color substitution.

Format selection recommendations:

  • Convert to WebP: Recommended for modern web applications and blogs. WebP offers both lossy and lossless modes, supporting transparency while cutting file sizes by up to 30% compared to JPG/PNG.
  • Convert to PNG: Best for app icons, UI graphics, logos, and illustrations where crisp pixel edges and transparent backgrounds must be preserved with lossless quality.
  • Convert to JPEG: Ideal for rich photographic assets, camera pictures, and complex textures where lossy compression delivers massive bandwidth savings.

Image Conversion FAQ

What happens to transparent backgrounds in JPEG?

Because JPEG does not support alpha channels, transparent pixels are replaced with your chosen background matte color (default is pure white).

Are image EXIF metadata tags preserved?

Metadata such as GPS coordinates and camera serials are stripped during export, protecting your privacy and slimming down file sizes.

Can I convert bulk images?

Yes. Select or drag multiple files into the workspace to convert them one after another in your local browser session.

Is the conversion performed on a remote server?

No. 100% of the conversion pipeline executes locally via the browser Canvas API, ensuring complete data privacy.
