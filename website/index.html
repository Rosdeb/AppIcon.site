Frequently Asked Questions About AppIcon

Everything you need to know about generating, sizing, and exporting app icons for iOS, Android, and desktop platforms.

What is AppIcon and how does the free app icon generator work?

AppIcon is an all-in-one free browser-based app icon generator. When you upload a high-resolution square graphic (recommended 1024×1024 px), AppIcon instantly generates all required icon sizes and formats for iOS (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch), Android (adaptive & mipmap densities), macOS (.icns), and Windows (.ico).

You can fine-tune background colors, adjust internal padding, test corner radius presets, preview your icon on realistic iPhone and Android device mockups, and download an organized ZIP file with zero cloud upload wait times.

What is the recommended master image size to upload for an app icon?

We recommend uploading a 1024×1024 pixel PNG image. Apple's App Store requires a 1024×1024 px icon with an opaque background (no alpha transparency), and Google Play requires a 512×512 px graphic. Starting with 1024×1024 ensures every downscaled resolution down to 16px maintains razor-sharp clarity and crisp typography without blur or pixelation.

Why does Apple reject iOS app icons with transparent backgrounds?

Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG) strictly forbid transparent pixels in iOS app icons. If an icon with alpha transparency is uploaded to App Store Connect, Apple's automated build verification will reject the binary with an ITMS-90717 error. AppIcon ensures your exported iOS icons have a solid background color so your app passes App Store review without delays.

How do I import the generated icons into Xcode or Android Studio?

For Xcode (iOS & macOS): Open your project in Xcode, navigate to Assets.xcassets, and simply drag the exported AppIcon.appiconset folder into the asset catalog. The included Contents.json manifest will automatically link all @1x, @2x, and @3x resolutions to their respective device slots.

For Android Studio: Open your app's app/src/main/res/ folder and copy the exported mipmap-mdpi, mipmap-hdpi, mipmap-xhdpi, mipmap-xxhdpi, and mipmap-xxxhdpi folders directly into it. Android will automatically load the appropriate density mipmap for each device screen.

Is AppIcon 100% free with complete privacy?

Yes! AppIcon is 100% free with no sign-up, no subscriptions, and no watermarks. Because our image processing pipeline operates entirely client-side using in-browser HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly, your confidential unreleased app graphics are never uploaded to remote cloud servers or third-party databases.
